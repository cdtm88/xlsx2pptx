How the rest is decided

Red, whatever the thresholds
An approver has rejected sign-off, or the sign-off date has passed without it.
Amber, whatever the thresholds
Open critical or high defects above the exit criteria, or a pass rate on the tests run so far below the pass target (once 5 or more have run).
Signed off
Every approver has approved, with or without conditions.
Not started
No tests in scope yet, or testing has not reached its start date.
Severity, in business terms
Critical: stops go-live, no workaround. High: a key process is impaired and the workaround is costly. Medium: a workaround exists and is acceptable for a while. Low: cosmetic or minor.
